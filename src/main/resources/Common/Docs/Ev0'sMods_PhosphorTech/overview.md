# Ev0's PhosphorTech

PhosphorTech is an industrial technology mod built around two independent power grids, multi-stage ore processing, a heat-driven steam system, and a modular fluid network.

---

## Tech Tree

Progress through four tiers to unlock increasingly powerful machines.

| Tier | Name | Power Source | Key Unlock |
|------|------|-------------|------------|
| **1** | Mechanical | Hand Crank / Windmill / Waterwheel | Sieve, Mechanical Grinder, Rod Puller |
| **2** | Conversion | Dynamo (J to CF) | Basic CF machines, Wire networks |
| **3** | Electrical | Crystal Generator (Steam to CF) | Crusher, Extractor, Centrifuge, Lathe |
| **4** | Advanced | Full CF network + Alloy Smelter | Steel production, Compressor, Pump |

---

## Progression Path

| Stage | You Have | You Can Build |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| Start | Nothing | Hand Crank, Shaft, Small Gear |
| Early | Gear network | Mechanical Grinder, Sieve, Rod Puller |
| Mid | Dynamo + Wires | Crusher, Extractor, Press, Lathe |
| Late | Crystal Generator | Centrifuge, Compressor, Alloy Smelter, Pump |
| End | Steel production | All advanced machines |

---

## Power at a Glance

| System | Unit | Carried By | Converted Via |
|--------|------|-----------|--------------|
| Mechanical | Joules (J) | Shafts and Gears | Dynamo converts J to CF |
| Electrical | Crystalline Flux (CF) | Wires | — |

> ! **1 Joule = 50 CF** via the Dynamo. Once a Crystal Generator is online (256 CF/tick) the Dynamo becomes a starting tool, not your main source.

---

## Quick Start

1. Craft a **Hand Crank** and place **Small Gears** alongside it
2. Connect a **Mechanical Grinder** to the gear network and crush your first ore
3. Smelt the resulting dust in a furnace to get bars
4. Build a **Dynamo** connected to both your gear network and Copper Wire to enter the CF era
5. Craft a **Steam Generator** and **Crystal Generator** for sustained high-output CF
6. Unlock **Steel** via the **Alloy Smelter** (Iron Bar + Coal Coke) to craft advanced machines
