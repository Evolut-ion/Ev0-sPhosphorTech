# Items

---

## Material Processing Chain

| Step | Machine | Input | Output |
|------|---------|-------|--------|
| 1 | Crusher or Mechanical Grinder | Raw Ore | Dust |
| 2 | Furnace | Dust | Bar |
| 3a | Press or Compressor | Bar | Plate |
| 3b | Lathe or Rod Puller | Bar | Rod |
| 3c | Alloy Smelter | 2x Bars | Alloy Bar |
| 4 | Crafting | Plates + Rods + Alloys | Advanced Components |

---

## Dusts

Output of the **Crusher** and **Mechanical Grinder**. Smelted in a furnace to produce bars.

| Category | Examples |
|----------|---------|
| Metals | Iron, Copper, Gold, Lead, Tin, Silver, Cobalt, Thorium |
| Minerals | Stone, Lime, Chalk, Marble, Quartz |

---

## Bars

| Bar | How to Obtain |
|-----|--------------|
| Iron Bar | Smelt Iron Dust |
| Copper Bar | Smelt Copper Dust |
| Stone Bar | Smelt Stone Dust |
| Gold Bar | Smelt Gold Dust |
| Steel Bar | Alloy Smelter: Iron Bar + Coal Coke |
