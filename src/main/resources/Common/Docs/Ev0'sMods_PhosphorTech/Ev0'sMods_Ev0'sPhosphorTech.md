# Ev0's PhosphorTech

PhosphorTech is an industrial technology mod that adds two independent power grids, a full suite of ore-processing machines, a fluid system with heat simulation, and multi-tier wiring and pipes.

## Quick Start

1. Craft a **Hand Crank** and connect it to **Small Gears**
2. Build a **Mechanical Grinder** to begin processing ores into dusts
3. Smelt dusts and raw materials in a furnace to obtain bars
4. Craft a **Crystal Generator** powered by steam to unlock the CF network
5. Use CF-powered machines â€” Crusher, Extractor, Press, Lathe â€” for more efficient processing
6. Wire machines together with **Copper Wire** (upgrade to higher tiers as your power needs grow)
